[Private Pilot — Account Required](https://app.testcloud.cc/no/jobs)

### Taleinnboks

[Live Application — Account Required](https://voice.norvix.no/login)

A mobile-first, offline-first voice inbox for craftspeople, service technicians and field-service teams.

Users record short spoken messages and the application turns them into structured tasks, reminders, material notes and work notes. AI-generated results are reviewed by the user before they are saved.

Taleinnboks is designed as a focused capture layer for field work rather than a replacement for an ERP, CRM or project-management system.

**Highlights:** voice capture, transcription, structured AI extraction, review before save, offline audio drafts, automatic synchronization, team workspaces, private attachments, reminders, usage metering and workspace export.

**Stack:** Next.js, React, TypeScript, Prisma, PostgreSQL, OpenAI API, Stripe, Sentry and Playwright.

**Status:** Deployed private-source product with controlled user access.
